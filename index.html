Sip Showdown - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Sip Showdown is a party game made by FusilliGames. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

  • Player names you type in at the start stay in the phone's memory while the app is open. They are not saved after you close the app and are never sent anywhere.
  • Settings (sound, vibration, pace, card packs, colour-blind mode and similar) are saved in a small file on your phone so they are remembered next time.
  • Cards you write yourself ("Your cards") are saved on your phone only.
  • A technical log file is kept on your phone to help fix crashes. It contains no personal information and never leaves the phone.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No online features: the game itself never connects to the internet. (Google Play's purchase system, used only for the unlock, may connect to Google.)
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell or share any information, because we do not have any.

Purchases

The app can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only learn from Google whether the unlock has been bought, so the app can switch the full game on.

Age

Sip Showdown is intended for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
